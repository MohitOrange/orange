On Foot & Ground

  • WASD — Move / drive
  • Mouse / Click — Aim & shoot
  • Space — Jump
  • Shift — Sprint (hold + W)
  • F — Enter / exit vehicle
  • E — Enter building / start mission
  • 1-5 — Switch weapon
  • Space — Handbrake (in car)
  • Walk off the beach to swim

Fly & Sail ✈🚁🚤

  • W/S — Forward / back
  • A/D — Turn (yaw)
  • Space — Ascend
  • Shift — Descend
